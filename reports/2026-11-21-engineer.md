any *other* cause still diagnoses; non-Quote failures are untouched.

other Quote failure → alert + diagnose; non-Quote failure → alert + diagnose. `python tools/engineer.py check`:
guard OK, 163 passed. Edited, pending the guard.

Net effect: these two tickets, and any future dark-connector day, still tell the user (binding reconnect item)
but no longer wake the Engineer at ~$2/day, and no longer inflate the autonomy metric.
