### 1. ON1 paper — day-20 outlook (agent/research/2026-10-31-weekly/on1_paper_day20_outlook.py)

**Hypothesis:** Is the dip to t=1.91 (Oct 29) structurally damaging, or is PASS still likely at day 20?

**Method:** Day-by-day breakdown (13 days), scenario analysis over next 7 days, break-even calculation using brentq, drop-one-day sensitivity.

**Variants:** 8 scenarios (avg daily bps from -50 to +90), drop-one for all 13 days.

**Day-by-day (day-level means):**
```
2026-10-13:  -34.1   2026-10-19:  +42.4   2026-10-23:  +52.2   2026-10-28:  -49.2
2026-10-14: +121.0   2026-10-20:  -17.4   2026-10-26:  +23.7   2026-10-29:  -81.0
2026-10-15:  +37.0   2026-10-21: +141.0   2026-10-27:  +67.6
2026-10-16: +311.6   2026-10-22:  +80.8
```
10/13 days positive; recent 2 days negative (-49.2 and -81.0).

**Key finding:** To reach t=2.0 at day 20, the next 7 days need only average **+7.2 bps**. Even flat (0 bps avg), t=1.84 — very close. The recent Oct 28-29 weakness is the main drag (drop-one: removing Oct 29 gives t=2.32; removing Oct 28 gives t=2.14). As those days age toward the back half of the sample, their weight on the overall t-stat decreases.

FAIL early trigger (mean < 0 at day >= 20) is essentially unreachable: mean is +53.5 bps; it would take catastrophic back-to-back losses to bring it below 0 in 7 days.

**Verdict:** ON1 paper is on track. PASS at day 20 is the modal outcome unless the next week produces consistent losses well below the current -81 bps floor.
