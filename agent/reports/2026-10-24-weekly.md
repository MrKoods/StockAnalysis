## Research

### 1. ON1 paper: is day-t +2.19 real or outlier-driven?

**Hypothesis before looking:** A single large positive day could produce t >2 at n=8.

**Method:** Day-by-day breakdown, drop-one t-stat for each of 8 days. (script: `agent/research/2026-10-24-weekly/on1_paper_analysis.py`)

**Results (8 scored days, 74 trades/day):**

| Day | Mean bps |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-13 | −34.1 |
| 2026-10-14 | +121.0 |
| 2026-10-15 | +37.0 |
| 2026-10-16 | **+311.6** (largest) |
| 2026-10-19 | +42.4 |
| 2026-10-20 | −17.4 |
| 2026-10-21 | +141.0 |
| 2026-10-22 | +80.8 |

Day-level: mean +85.3 bps, std 109.9 bps, t=+2.19. 6/8 days positive.

Drop-one t-stats: dropping the largest day (Oct 16) still gives t=**2.13**. Dropping Oct 14, Oct 21, or Oct 22 each drops t below 2.0 (range 1.76–1.91). The other 4 drops leave t≥2.

**Verdict:** Not a single-outlier artifact — removing the largest day preserves t. However 3/8 drop-ones fall below t=2.0, confirming the signal is fragile at n=8 as expected. 12+ more days needed before evaluation window opens at day 20.

### 2. V2 rank: what predicts time-stop vs loss exit?

**Hypothesis before looking:** Stocks in lower VIX, or with positive momentum, or lower RSI might trend better and more often hit the 15-day time-stop rather than stop-loss.

**Method:** Mann-Whitney U tests on VIX, RSI, rs_zscore, mom_5d, technical_score, confidence for time_stop (n=26) vs loss (n=27). Also direction breakdown. (scripts: `agent/research/2026-10-24-weekly/rank_timestop_analysis.py`, `rank_timestop_analysis2.py`)

**Results:**

| Feature | Time-stop mean | Loss mean | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| vix_at_signal | 15.74 | 15.39 | 0.116 |
| rsi_14 | 41.6 | 47.4 | 0.124 |
| rs_zscore | −0.60 | −0.10 | 0.203 |
| mom_5d | −0.011 | +0.009 | **0.040** |
| technical_score | 31.4 | 32.6 | 0.104 |
| confidence | 60.0 | 58.8 | 0.735 |

Direction: bearish time-stops 54.5% (18/33), bullish 40.0% (8/20), p=0.31.

Only mom_5d reaches p<0.05, but with 6 features tested this is expected by chance (1 in 6 = 16.7% false-positive rate). The direction result is in the intuitive direction but not significant. All 56 closed trades are options-era; shares-era has no closures yet.

**Verdict:** No robust predictor of time-stop vs loss at current n. The outcome appears to be random short-term price movement, not something the model's logged features can pre-sort. This is consistent with the earlier finding that the time-stop provides the only positive R contribution. Cannot change the live experiment; useful context for a future track design if this one fails at n=100.
