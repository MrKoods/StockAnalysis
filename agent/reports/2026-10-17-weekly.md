### 2. V2 rank track: time-stop attribution (agent/research/2026-10-17-rank-timestop/)

**Question:** Is the rank track's near-zero mean R entirely driven by the 15-day time-stop exits, or is there broader edge?

**Pre-stated hypothesis:** Time-stop exits contribute positively (positions that moved toward the target but didn't reach 3R, held 15 days and exited at intermediate gains); losses drag. If time stops explain all positive R, there is no formal-target edge.

**Method:** Filtered rank_trades.csv to 53 closed trades (outcomes: time_stop n=25, loss n=25, win n=2, earnings_exit n=1). Computed mean achieved_rr per group and a contribution decomposition.

**Results by outcome:**

| Outcome | n | Mean R | t |
|---|---|---|---|
| time_stop | 25 | **+0.818R** | +3.56 |
| loss | 25 | **−1.105R** | −27.0 |
| win | 2 | **+3.927R** | +35.9 |
| earnings_exit | 1 | −0.139R | — |

**Contribution decomposition:**

| Bucket | R sum | Share of total |
|---|---|---|
| Time-stop exits (n=25) | +20.45R | 3779% |
| Non-time-stop exits (n=28) | −19.91R | −3679% |
| **Net (all 53)** | **+0.54R** | 100% |

Mean R all closed: **+0.010R** (matches snapshot). Mean R without time-stop exits: **−0.711R**.

Time-stop direction split: bearish n=17 (mean +0.795R), bullish n=8 (mean +0.867R). Both contribute positively. Mean holding days at time stop: 11.6 days (range 10–15).

**Verdict:** Confirmed. The rank track's entire positive R comes from the 15-day time stop. The pattern is: ~47% of closed trades exit at time stop (~+0.818R average — positions moving the right direction but not far enough for 3R); ~47% hit the full stop-loss (~−1.105R); these nearly cancel. The formal 3R target was hit only twice in 53 trades. This is consistent with the prior finding (v2.2.128) that MFE averages 1.3x ATR vs a ~6x-ATR 3R target.

**Implication for go/no-go at n=100:** The time stop is acting as a soft exit at ~+0.818R, not the formal 3R target. If the track eventually passes go/no-go, the user should understand the "edge" is a small mean-reversion captured by a 15-day exit — not consistent target achievement. This does not change the frozen rule or the experiment; it is context for interpreting the eventual result.

**Variants tried:** 1 (decomposition by outcome). Alternative exit rules were not tested (that would be tuning a live experiment).
